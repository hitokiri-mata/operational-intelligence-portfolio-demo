# AvantStay — AI-built product example

Over the past year, I've been building an AI-assisted operational intelligence dashboard to make scattered staffing, service, and financial information easier to understand and act on.

I started with a simple challenge: leaders often have the data, but not a clear way to connect it to decisions. I personally defined the product problem, key metrics, business rules, dashboard structure, filters, drill-downs, and user experience. I used AI coding tools to accelerate prototyping and explore alternative interface designs, while keeping ownership of the product decisions, testing, and validation of the numbers.

What matters most to me is not adding AI for the sake of it. It's making complicated work simpler and giving people information they can trust.

The original project involves private operational data, so I've prepared a separate interactive portfolio demonstration with fictional clients and synthetic numbers. It illustrates the product experience without exposing any confidential information.
